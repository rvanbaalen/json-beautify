JSON Beautifier

Paste or type JSON and format it with one click. Choose a preset such as two-space, four-space, tab-indented, compact minified output, or sorted keys, then copy the result to your clipboard. Invalid JSON is reported with the parser error so you can find the problem quickly, and you can compare two documents to see how they differ. Everything is processed locally in your browser, so no data ever leaves your machine.
